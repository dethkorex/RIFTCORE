# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.17

- Ember Foundry has new routes, traps, and a rising lava escape. Defeat Kiln Warden and Forge Titan by opening their armor during their attacks.
- Machine Jungle has canopy launches and vine swings over open chasms. Find both gate relays in each act to unlock its boss room.
- Briar Sentinel and Verdant Colossus have new models, intro cinematics, and clearer attack effects. Break Briar's seed anchors and activate Verdant's canopy drains to expose their cores.
- Machine Jungle enemies start at Lv. 4 on Normal. Both Jungle bosses have more health, deal more damage, and resist more damage as difficulty increases.
- This update keeps your account and saves.

Update through Launcher 0.2 or Settings → Update Game before joining a party.
