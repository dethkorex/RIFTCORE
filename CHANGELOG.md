# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.25

- The opening cinematic waits for Next on each panel so you can read at your own pace.
- The Operations Hub stations now share a consistent size and style. New end walls and floor panels close gaps at both ends of the hub.
- Reworked campaign routes, including new cavern branches and relay return paths, an expanded Ember Foundry lava escape and Abyssal Plant’s submerged pressure-valve route.
- Fixed the floor seam before the first Foundry jump and aligned Verdant Colossus’s east drain with its platform.
- Charged buster shots now register against the visible shields of regular enemies. Rolling boulders continue past warning markers until they hit terrain or are destroyed.
- Claim completed challenge rewards from the Challenges station. Reserve lives now display in the total, and early shop items and Core Shard payouts have been rebalanced.

Update through Launcher 0.2 or Settings → Update Game. Windows and Linux builds are available.
