# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.1.4

- Every sector alternates between open-air stretches and enclosed routes. Cross factory catwalks, ice ridges, launch decks, and orbital walkways before heading back indoors.
- Machine Jungle restores the outdoor panorama and leads from the canopy into the Root Caves and Hollow Trunk.
- Cave arches and boss doors face the direction of travel, with the moving panels aligned to their blockers.
- All 20 acts have more jumps, spike pits, and high routes that reconnect with the main path.
- Boss and miniboss rooms have distinct layouts with dodge lifts, projectile cover, currents, and other ways to use the arena.
- Refreshed sector decking, wall panels, lighting, and biome machinery.

Windows and Linux players can update through the launcher or Settings → Update Game.
