# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.11

- The Operator station groups Gear, Storage, and operator slots. Visit the separate Profile station for your career, cosmetics, and account settings.
- Open Find Friends from the Friends list. Friend requests and game invites share one Notifications tab.
- The challenge countdown is larger and appears above the challenge panels. In-game chat keeps its keyboard hints and composer visible.
- The old in-game leaderboards are retired. The live player directory remains available through Find Friends.
- This playtest includes a one-time account and save reset. Create a new account or continue offline. Future progress resets preserve accounts and the RIFTCORE friends list.

Update through Launcher 0.2 or Settings → Update Game before joining a party.
