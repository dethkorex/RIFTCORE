# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.40

## Ember Foundry
- Act I now winds through basalt corridors, upper gantries and lower salvage routes. Explore for the two gate relays, hidden caches and optional field module.
- Rolling boulders move more slowly and break in three standard buster shots or two charged shots.
- Act II links its fuel trench, crane platforms and refinery through wider jumps. Both relays must be active before the lava escape opens.
- The escape shaft is wider, with fewer ledges, four marked wall-kick sections and three flying scouts. The lava rises 15% faster. Escape onto the roof and cross the exhaust pier to Forge Titan.
- Lava pools extend below the floor, and the upper foundry skyline follows the climb.

## Saber combat
- Switch to Saber Mode to keep the blade drawn, move 10% faster and block incoming attacks from the front. The fire button swings the saber on the ground or in the air.
- Hold fire to charge a saber wave. Suit-colored sparks and the drawn blade show your charge, including in co-op.

## Nova and co-op
- The opening cinematic offers direct access to Nova’s station tour. Solo field lessons cover Act I movement, combat and exploration.
- Nova explains your first material and blueprint. Your first quick-slot supply opens Inventory with the item highlighted so you can assign it to a D-pad slot. Each lesson can be skipped.
- Co-op skips the field tutorials. Running out of lives opens operator selection for your next deployment. A teammate can still reboot your current operator.
