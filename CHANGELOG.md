# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.1

- Bosses and minibosses get longer entrances with close-ups, portrait dialogue, and an overhead name reveal, a heavier six-track metal rotation, and larger numbered health bars.
- Every sector recommends an operator level. Hard improves loot, and finishing every sector on Hard unlocks Nightmare and its exclusive boss blueprints.
- Select a blueprint to learn it or stash it in the eight-slot Blueprint Pouch. You can drop blueprints for party members in the hub.
- Use LB and RB to browse Shop and Equipment categories. Incoming damage numbers are easier to read.
- The game opens with an animated Destructive Force Gaming splash, custom audio, and a smoother loading screen.
- New operators meet NOVA at the Deploy pad. The first full defeat introduces reconstruction.
- Delete saved operators from the title menu. Faster ladder exits and more reliable platform collisions improve traversal.

Windows and Linux players can update through the launcher or Settings → Update Game.
