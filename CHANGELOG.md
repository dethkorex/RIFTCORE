# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.9

- First launch lets you create an account, sign in, or continue offline. After creating an account, sign in with your email and password.
- Gear shows the character preview, Operator Level, and stats together.
- Storage shows Inventory on the left and Storage on the right. Select an inventory stack to move it across.
- The Equipment screen now has two tabs: Gear and Storage. Unlearned blueprints from the old pouch appear in Storage.

Update through Launcher 0.2 or Settings → Update Game before joining a party.
