# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.49

## Progression and blueprints
- Core Shard balances, rewards and Ascension prices use whole numbers. Existing fractional balances round up.
- Mission clears award half the Profile XP they paid before Alpha 6.3.44. A Normal Ember Foundry clear awards 88 Profile XP, down from 257 in Alpha 6.3.44. Existing Profile XP and Operator XP are preserved.
- Daily challenge progress resets on upgrade. Claimed rewards stay in your account.
- Standard challenges award 3–5 Core Shards. Harder challenges award 10–15. Set completion still pays its own bonus.
- Common blueprints use 3 Industrial Scrap and one locally available component. Higher rarities scale up with gear quality. Epic and Legendary recipes still use Boss Cores, and Legendary recipes require a Fracture Crystal.
- Fabricated sector gear now gives stronger bonuses. Early Forge plans offer 8% damage resistance, +14 maximum HP, or +10% Buster and saber damage, depending on the plan.

## Combat and loot
- Buster aim tracks a continuous arc. Aim downward during free fall, fire left or right on ladders, and use crouch shots on the ground.
- Salvage chests and boss caches have an 8% chance to drop a 1UP. Co-op rolls a personal drop for each operator.

## Ember Foundry Act I
- Moved the slag boulder onto the far slope so it rolls away from the midpoint checkpoint.
- Removed the decorative rock shelf that obscured the Coolant Well crossing and ladder approach.

## Display
- Display mode, resolution, V-Sync, frame limit and graphics quality stay pending until you choose Apply and Accept.

## Mission HUD and co-op extraction
- The in-game HUD pairs the minimap with the objective, wallet and timer. Squad rows show names, health, remaining lives and off-screen direction separately.
- Co-op uses the solo Extraction Summary with each operator’s own items, XP, defeats and lives lost. Return to the hub whenever you close your summary. Each teammate returns when they close theirs.

## Operations Hub
- Walk over a party drop to pick it up. Pickups use the player's position at the time of contact, so they work while moving between network updates. Party members can trade a spare blueprint even after learning the recipe.
- Blueprint, material and supply drops use distinct glowing pickups. Blueprint drops show a schematic icon in the hub and in missions.
- Tab and controller Select open carried Inventory and Quick Slots. Drop carried items for your party from this screen.
- To withdraw items, visit Operator → Storage and choose Move to Inventory. The Inventory shortcut leaves the Operator station tabs out of the menu.
