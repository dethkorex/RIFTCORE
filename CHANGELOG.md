# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.55

## Operator progression
- Operator levels grant stronger base damage and damage resistance. The damage bonus also applies to the saber, boss suit weapons, and their damage effects.
- At Operator Level 10, base weapon damage is 99% above Level 1 and level-based defense reduces incoming damage by about 31% before gear and buffs.
- Existing operators automatically use the new rates from their saved Operator XP.
- Core Ascension health and energy talents now scale with maximum capacity. Several defensive, charge, and Scavenger talents also grant stronger bonuses. Existing ranks use the new effects automatically.

## Workshop and cloud saves
- Crafting has a filterable recipe list, a schematic preview with material requirements, and a Materials in Storage panel.
- Marketplace pairs a filterable catalog with item stats, distinct item icons, quantity controls, and a live purchase total.
- Marketplace prices reflect item strength and rarity. Stock up at a 10% discount for five purchases or 15% for ten, with a Max button for the largest affordable order.
- Core Ascension has color-coded talent branches, clearer tier connections and ranks, and a focused panel for effects and shard costs.
- Checking Cloud Saves shows the active stage, connected profile, last recorded sync, and the number of cloud saves once verified.

## Salvage and blueprints
- Caches split salvage across the parts used in each sector instead of dropping large stacks of the same two materials. Material drop chances improve with Operator Level and difficulty.
- Regular caches have higher blueprint chances on all three difficulties. Boss caches still guarantee a blueprint and a Boss Core, with better odds for scarce materials on Hard and Expert.
- Recipe costs follow rarity and equipment type. Common plans use Scrap and one part, Rare plans add an alloy or another part, Epic plans require Boss Cores, and Legendary plans also require a Fracture Crystal.
