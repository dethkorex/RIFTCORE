# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.39

## Soundtrack
- Ten sectors now have their own exploration themes, with a separate track for every mini-boss and main boss.
- Music crossfades into boss fights and back to the sector theme when the fight ends.

## Opening and Operations Hub
- Removed the standalone Mission 01 Unlocked screen. The opening now continues straight into Operations Hub.
- NOVA gives RX-07 more room during the station tour.

## Ember Foundry Act I
- Pick a run module at the new terminal near deployment. Your choice carries into Act II.
- Take the upper gantry for the Forge Reactive Liner blueprint or the lower vent route for a Patch Kit. New basalt formations frame the furnace and sump.
- Rolling boulders travel past the yellow markers and shatter on obstacles. Six standard Buster hits break a boulder.

Update through the RIFTCORE Launcher on Windows or Linux.
