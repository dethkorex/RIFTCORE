# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.46

## Progression and blueprints
- Mission clears award half the Profile XP they paid before Alpha 6.3.44. A Normal Ember Foundry clear awards 88 Profile XP, down from 257 in Alpha 6.3.44. Existing Profile XP and Operator XP are preserved.
- Standard challenges award 3–5 Core Shards. Harder challenges award 10–15. Set completion still pays its own bonus.
- Common blueprints use 3 Industrial Scrap and one locally available component. Higher rarities scale up with gear quality. Epic and Legendary recipes still use Boss Cores, and Legendary recipes require a Fracture Crystal.
- Fabricated sector gear now gives stronger bonuses. Early Forge plans offer 8% damage resistance, +14 maximum HP, or +10% Buster and saber damage, depending on the plan.

## Operations Hub
- Tab and controller Select open carried Inventory and Quick Slots. Drop carried items for your party from this screen.
- To withdraw items, visit Operator → Storage and choose Move to Inventory. The Inventory shortcut leaves the Operator station tabs out of the menu.
