# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.1

- Bosses and minibosses get cinematic entrances, six rotating metal tracks, and numbered health bars.
- Every sector recommends an operator level. Hard improves loot, and finishing every sector on Hard unlocks Nightmare and its exclusive boss blueprints.
- Select a blueprint to learn it or stash it in the eight-slot Blueprint Pouch. You can drop blueprints for party members in the hub.
- Use LB and RB to browse Shop and Equipment categories. Incoming damage numbers are easier to read.
- The game opens with a new animated Destructive Force Gaming splash.

Windows and Linux players can update through the launcher or Settings → Update Game.
