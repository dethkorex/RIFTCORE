# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.56

## Campaign combat

- Rebalanced regular enemies, Act I minibosses, and Act II main bosses across all ten sectors on Normal, Hard, and Expert.
- Sector and difficulty drive enemy strength, so higher-level operators keep their advantage when revisiting earlier sectors. Act II patrols have 18% more HP than their Act I counterparts.
- Minibosses and main bosses have separate HP curves. Their attacks gain 10% and 15% power respectively in addition to sector and difficulty scaling.

## Co-op

- Each guest adds 30% enemy HP and 45% boss HP. A hit deals the same damage as it does in solo at the same difficulty.
- The existing 50% shared Operator XP bonus gains another 25 percentage points per guest. Two-, three-, and four-player squads earn 175%, 200%, and 225% of base XP respectively. Partial XP carries forward between rewards and acts.
