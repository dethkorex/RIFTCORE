# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.1.10

- Neon Factory becomes Ember Foundry. Both acts have new fire-and-machinery art, reworked enemies, larger flame vents, clearer entrances, and fewer redundant platforms.
- Shoot floating power couplers to operate machinery. Break the rolling slag boulder with 10 regular shots or 4 charged shots. Time jumps over pit launchers that fire upward.
- Earn XP immediately from kills. In co-op, the party shares kill XP and boss powers, and each operator collects their own drops.
- Rare enemy drops contain health, energy or Bolts. Supply drones carry health, energy or a rare quick-use item. Search chests for materials, blueprints and quick-use gear. Each final boss cache guarantees a blueprint, materials and a quick-use item. Blueprint drops follow operator level, sector and difficulty.
- Fight the new Kiln Warden miniboss and a rebuilt Forge Titan. Boss warning markers are easier to read, and all 10 sectors have themed doorways.
- Cannon shots and charged saber waves travel farther. Controller bindings and the pause inventory are easier to navigate, with equipment icons and recovery feedback.
- Challenges display their 24-hour countdown and award more Core Shards, including a bonus for clearing the full set.
- Selecting an operator moves focus to its actions. Press B or Escape to go back to the roster.
- The Social Hub has searchable contacts, pinned friends, party whispers, quick messages and an invite inbox. Copy a room code in one action, or invite a Steam friend when Steam presence is available.

Co-op players should update together. Windows and Linux players can update through the launcher or Settings → Update Game.
