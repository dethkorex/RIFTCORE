# RIFTCORE Alpha 6.3.21

- Creating an account signs you in automatically and opens operator selection. A failed automatic sign-in stays in the same form with a retry button; it never submits another registration for the account just created.
- Incorrect login details keep the sign-in screen open and show the error. Successful sign-in closes it and continues to operator selection.

- One player reaching a checkpoint now saves it for the whole squad. Every party member receives the checkpoint notification; reconnecting preserves it without repeating the banner.
- Every co-op player can revive a fallen teammate by holding Field Interact beside their body. A progress bar fills during the hold; releasing, moving away or taking damage interrupts it.
- Revived teammates return at their body with full HP and no extra lives. Bodies lost to a fall return to a safe platform, and bodies follow moving platforms. Field Reboot talents shorten the revive hold.
- Revived operators with no spare lives can continue into the next act and extract normally. A complete squad wipe still returns everyone to the hub together and keeps the party open.
- Fixed stationary guest-position drift after rapid movement and delayed network acknowledgements. Compact teammate updates now include rocket-board, level and status-effect visuals.

- Frozen Reactor has new Rime Keeper and Cryo Warden models, with mechanical rigs and animation poses that keep their armor, cannon and shield rigid.
- Frozen Reactor has updated ice surfaces, grippy decking, icicles, coolant machinery and frost effects for enemy and boss attacks.
- Act II adds the Rocket Spillway: a course of more than 90 seconds with downhill momentum, upward rocket thrust, jump pads, gaps and crouch gates. Complete it to activate Gate Relay A and return to the midway checkpoint.
- The rocket board has a shaped hull, twin engines, fins and lighting. It hovers above the track, follows slopes and has clearance beneath the raised spike gates when crouched.
- Co-op synchronizes each player's board and boss animations. Joining or restoring a rider on the spillway also restores the guest's camera bounds.
- NOVA and the tutorial manual explain the rocket course and its controls.
- Full-resolution GPU-compressed Frozen boss textures reduce video-memory use. Duplicate editor texture sheets are excluded from downloads to keep this update compatible with existing launchers.
- This update keeps your account and saves.

Both players should update through Launcher 0.2 or Settings → Update Game before joining a party.
