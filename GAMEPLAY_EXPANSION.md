# RIFTCORE gameplay expansion

The next overhaul can use the station as a place to make choices before a run, and Act II as the place those choices pay off.

| Feature | How it plays |
| --- | --- |
| Optional salvage routes | Take the direct route to the boss or detour through a harder puzzle for bolts, a stowable item, or a repair cache. |
| Gear tradeoffs | Add equipment that changes your options, such as faster air dashes with a lower energy cap. Show the tradeoff on the inventory card before purchase. |
| Route briefings | The Deploy pad shows the upcoming sector hazard, your equipped slots, and one short hint from the previous act. |
| Boss puzzle remixes | Teach an interaction in Act I, test it under pressure in Act II, and let the player use it for a short opening against the boss. |
| Elite contracts | Take a station contract to add a tougher enemy with a visible modifier and a guaranteed piece of gear. |
| Boss practice | Replay defeated bosses at the station with a chosen suit and instant retries. |
| Timed extraction | Add an optional escape route after a boss clear. Reach a second extraction pad before it locks for a rarity bonus. |
| Route variants | Rotate a few enemy placements and optional cache rooms between runs. Keep the required puzzle cues consistent so players can learn them. |

## Sector puzzles

| Sector | Puzzle | Act II escalation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · Neon Factory | Swap the direction of conveyor lines with clearly marked switches. | Reverse a moving lift while dodging presses to reach a high service corridor. |
| 02 · Machine Jungle | Open sap valves to grow climbable vines toward a chosen exit. | Time the growth around retracting thorns and falling seed pods. |
| 03 · Frozen Reactor | Route coolant to freeze selected jets into stepping stones. | The first frozen steps thaw while the next ones form, creating a timed climb. |
| 04 · Abyssal Plant | Flood or drain adjacent chambers to float crates into place. | Change water levels while swimming through a circuit that alternates which lane is electrified. |
| 05 · Buried Archives | Match glyphs on walls to switch between two sets of visible ledges. | Activate the next glyph during a jump as your current platform phases out. |
| 06 · Sky Dock | Aim turbines to redirect air lanes across gaps. | Chain a wall jump and air dash between alternating gusts to land on a moving dock. |
| 07 · BioLab | Feed growth chambers to make temporary bounce pads. | Choose the bloom order to reach a vent before the pads wilt and the spores settle. |
| 08 · Orbital Array | Flip gravity in bounded rooms using clearly lit terminals. | Jump between floor and ceiling during gaps in the solar sweep. |
| 09 · Scrapyard Expanse | Change magnetic polarity to move scrap blocks into footholds. | Build a climbing route while a press resets blocks left in its lane. |
| 10 · RiftCore Nexus | Redirect power through relays to open paths for a few seconds. | Chain three relay rooms that combine phase ledges, gravity changes, and a timed escape. |

Start with Neon Factory and Orbital Array. The conveyor puzzle tests readable timing, and the gravity puzzle tests an entirely different movement rule. Give each a safe practice room and a short retry before applying the same pattern across the campaign.
