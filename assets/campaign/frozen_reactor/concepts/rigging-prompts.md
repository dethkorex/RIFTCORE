# Built-in image generation reference prompts

These use the approved Rime Keeper and Cryo Warden concepts as edit targets.

## Shared prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Produce a single full body FRONT VIEW T-POSE reference of this exact existing game robot for 3D reconstruction and rigging. Preserve character identity and style exactly: high quality detailed stylized 3D game art, navy blue and white armor, cyan coolant details, lightly frosted edges. Change pose and camera only. Two feet apart flat on ground, symmetrical skeleton, no bent arms, all limbs separated with white space, no limb intersecting torso, all extremities visible with white margin. Pure white background, no text, no inset, no extra characters. Preserve original robot design; don't invent new weapons or parts.

## Rime Keeper subject constraint

Preserve very squat compact proportions, extremely short legs, broad rectangular shoulders, oversized vented gauntlets, tiny black two-finger grippers. No shield or spear. Arms held horizontally out at shoulder height in a T pose, grippers clear of hips.

## Cryo Warden subject constraint

Preserve taller brass piston legs, large white shoulders, two separate back coolant tanks, tiny narrow helmet, cyan circular chest core, right forearm ice cannon and left forearm kite shield. Arms in a horizontal T pose with ice cannon pointing outward on viewer left, shield attached on outside of left forearm on viewer right, shield long axis follows the forearm horizontally in this rigging pose; grip and hand behind shield. Shield well clear of torso and legs.

## Shield correction

Change only the shield placement and attachment on the robot's left arm (viewer right). Its exterior armored face faces the camera and slightly outward. Hand, wrist, handle and brackets sit behind the solid armored face. Remove the visible front hand and grip and continue matching navy armor with cyan illuminated inset details. Keep the shield below the elbow, clear of the leg and above the ground. Preserve the robot, opposing cannon, palette, rendering, camera, and white background.
